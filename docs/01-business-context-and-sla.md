# Business Context & SLA Specification — FinancialIntentional.com

## 1. Project Overview
- **Client:** Robert Wroblewski (`FinancialIntentional.com`)
- **Agency / Developer:** Hemant (Evexta Innovations)
- **Primary Objective:** Transition from a monolithic multi-page WordPress build to a high-performance, mobile-first Single-Page Application (SPA) personal brand hub focused on Mortgage Lead Generation, supported by Financial Advisory (Treece Financial Group) and Corporate Recruiting (JSS Recruiting).

## 2. Commercial Terms & Milestone Schedule
- **Base Contract Value:** $1,775 (Subject to Calculator Complexity Caveat below)
- **Payment Milestone Structure (40 / 25 / 25 / 10):**
  1. **40% Kickoff & Discovery ($710):** Due upon contract execution and initial asset handoff.
  2. **25% Prototype & UI/UX Sign-Off ($443.75):** Due upon approval of the single-page layout, Vercel staging preview, and interactive UI flow (Client Review Round 1).
  3. **25% Functional & Calculator Completion ($443.75):** Due upon completion of 1:1 Excel calculator integration, Headless CMS hookup, and pre-launch functional review.
  4. **10% Production Launch & Handoff ($177.50):** Due upon GoDaddy DNS cutover to Vercel, live verification, and completion of the 90-minute training session.

## 3. Revision Policy & Governance
- **Design Revisions:** Strictly scoped to **2 structured design rounds** following initial prototype presentation.
- **Functional Review:** **1 comprehensive pre-launch functional QA review** prior to production DNS cutover.
- **Intellectual Property & Ownership:** Robert Wroblewski retains **100% ownership** of the website, domain name, custom calculator source code, and content assets upon receipt of the final 10% milestone payment.

## 4. Post-Launch Maintenance & SLA (90 Days)
- **Coverage Window:** 90 calendar days following production DNS cutover.
- **SLA Response Times:**
  - **Critical / Site-Down or Lead-Capture Failure (Severity 1):** 48-hour maximum response and remediation initiation window.
  - **Minor UI / Content Rendering Bug (Severity 2):** 5-business-day resolution window.
- **Scope Exclusions:** New feature development, Phase 2 CRM API integrations (HubSpot/Salesforce), or modifications to Excel calculation logic after final sign-off are scoped separately.
